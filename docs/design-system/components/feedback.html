Not this

✗ console.error('Export failed', e) — the button finishes, no file arrives, and the analyst is told nothing.

Errors say what broke and what to do next; they do not apologise and are never vague. A failure message never becomes the content it failed to produce — error text written into a report body is savable and exportable as though it were the product.
